[low, a newsroom voice, matter-of-fact] For sixteen years, the Swedish taxpayer funded an organisation tied to the Muslim Brotherhood. Not secretly. On the books, with a receipt. [patient, itemising] Ibn Rushd was one of ten study associations recognised by the Swedish state. From 2008 to 2024 it drew state grants on the order of twenty-nine million kronor a year. Its ties to the Brotherhood were raised again, and again, and again — and every single time, Sweden commissioned another report instead of closing the tap. [clipped, procedural] In 2023 the funding body finally looked properly. It found study material incompatible with the terms of the grant. In 2024 it cut every krona for the next three years. Ibn Rushd shut down in 2025. [pause] [slow, each word landing] Sixteen years. The state only moved once the thing it had paid for was finished. [dry] And it is still going. A government inquiry into banning foreign money for religious organisations reports this May. One case in the file: a Shia mosque outside Stockholm, flagged for working with the Iranian regime. [quiet, final] Sweden was not infiltrated. Sweden wrote the cheques. [pause] [warmer, direct, asking] Should any of it have been public money? Comments.
