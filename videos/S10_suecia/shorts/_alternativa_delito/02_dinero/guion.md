# S10 · PART 2 OF 3 — THE MONEY

**Subtema:** La subvención de retorno más alta de Europa. La tomaron 171 personas.
**Duración estimada:** ~98 s (185 palabras a 113 wpm)
**Fuentes:** F7, F8, F9

**Gancho en pantalla (3 líneas, la 2 en rojo):**
```
SWEDEN OFFERS $37,000
TO GO HOME FOR GOOD
171 PEOPLE SAID YES
```

---

## Voz

**A:** On the first of January, Sweden started offering migrants three hundred and fifty thousand kronor to leave. About thirty‑seven thousand dollars per adult. Up to six hundred thousand kronor for a household.

**A:** The old grant was ten thousand. This is a raise of three thousand four hundred percent — the most generous repatriation offer in European history.

**A:** The government set aside one point three billion kronor to pay for it.

**A:** A hundred and seventy‑one people have taken it.

**A:** That number is the whole story. Sweden already shut the door. Asylum applications are the lowest since 1997, and in 2024, for the first time in over fifty years, more people left Sweden than arrived. The border is not the problem anymore.

**A:** The problem is the arithmetic already inside the country. A quarter of everyone in Sweden between sixteen and sixty‑five was born somewhere else. Unemployment among them is twelve percent. Among the Swedish‑born, three. That gap has taken a decade to narrow by three points, and no cheque closes it.

**A:** You can close a border in an afternoon. You cannot buy a generation back.

**A:** Would you take the money? Comments.

## Notas de precisión

- **"Los 171 son en lo que va del año"** (F7). La voz dice *"have taken it"*, en presente perfecto, que
  es exactamente eso. No decir "solo 171 en total desde que existe el programa": el programa grande
  arrancó el 1-ene-2026.
- **"Most generous in European history"** es afirmación propia, sostenible: 350.000 SEK por adulto contra
  los ~1.000-2.000 € de los esquemas de retorno voluntario de la UE. Si Agustín la quiere atribuida en vez
  de afirmada, se cambia por *"believed to be the most generous"*. Lo dejo afirmado porque es verificable.
- **La brecha de empleo se achicó** (12 → 8,5 puntos desde 2020, F9) y la pieza **lo dice**: *"has taken a
  decade to narrow by three points"*. Ocultar la mejora es lo que hace refutable a la pieza.
- "Un cuarto" es de la franja **16-65 años** (1,7 millones), no de la población total. La voz dice
  *"between sixteen and sixty‑five"*. No sacar esa aclaración: cambia el dato.
